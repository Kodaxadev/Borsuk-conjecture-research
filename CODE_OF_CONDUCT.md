# Code of Conduct

This repository is a research collaboration space. Contributions should be intellectually rigorous, respectful, and evidence-driven.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussion.
- Distinguish between a mathematical claim and a package-local artifact.
- Preserve reproducibility and clear status labeling.
- Favor precise, verifiable statements over unsupported speculation.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or abusive conduct.
- Publicly undermining a claim without pointing to the underlying evidence.
- Introducing unverifiable or misleading package-level claims.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or reject contributions that fail to meet the repository's research and collaboration standards.
