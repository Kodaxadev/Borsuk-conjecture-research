# Research roadmap

## Goal

Establish a coherent mathematical research repository for finite-combinatorial work around the 0/1-Borsuk conjecture.

## Near-term priorities

1. Create stable package-level provenance for each candidate and witness package.
2. Normalize verification instructions across the subdirectories.
3. Maintain a project-level status ledger for what is known, what is conjectural, and what remains unresolved.
4. Add reproducibility and auditability metadata where appropriate.

## Long-term priorities

- standardize naming conventions for witness and proof artifacts,
- provide a central cross-package verification checklist,
- track reduction lemmas and dependency graphs in a consistent way,
- support downstream publication-quality summarization.
